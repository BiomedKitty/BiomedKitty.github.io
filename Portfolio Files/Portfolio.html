About me

I am currently a student at the University of North Dakota pursuing a double major in Biomedical and Electrical Engineering, with a minor in Computer Science. With over 6 years of experience as an EMT, nearly a year as a Certified Surgical Technologist (CST), and coursework in Paramedic Science, I have a vast understanding of the medical field as well as an in depth knowledge of human anatomy and physiology. My journey through these roles, particularly in the operating room, sparked my fascination with developing enhanced medical devices. This experience introduced me to surgical implants and instruments, igniting my passion to innovate solutions benefiting patients, surgeons, and surgical technologists alike. It was during my studies toward CST and Paramedic that I found I had an undeniable fascination and inclination for the cardiovascular system. My ultimate aspiration is to contribute to the field of cardiovascular engineering and play a part in pioneering advanced cardiac care technologies.
